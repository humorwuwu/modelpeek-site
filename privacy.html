隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-11 · 适用于 ModelPeek iPhone / iPad 应用

ModelPeek（以下简称「本应用」）是一款离线 3D / CAD 模型查看器。我们尽量少地处理你的信息： 本应用不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。

我们处理什么

  • 你选择的模型文件：用于在设备本地转换和显示。文件会被复制到本应用的临时目录， 转换结果缓存在应用的 Caches 目录中。你可以随时在应用内清除缓存，或删除应用来清除全部数据。
  • 应用设置与审查批注：保存在设备本地（UserDefaults 与应用沙盒），不会上传。 审查批注只有在你主动导出/分享时才会离开设备。
  • 崩溃与诊断日志：仅保存在设备本地，用于排查问题；本应用不会自动上传。

我们不做什么

  • 不要求注册或登录；
  • 不收集设备标识符、位置、通讯录、照片或使用行为；
  • 不包含广告、分析或追踪 SDK；
  • 不向第三方服务器发送模型或任何文件内容；
  • 不进行跨应用或跨网站追踪。

网络

本应用不需要网络即可完成转换和查看。应用内部通过自定义 URL scheme 读取随包分发的 查看器资源，不访问互联网。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童收集任何数据，也不包含广告或用户生成内容。

数据保留

模型缓存和本地设置保存在你的设备上，直到你在应用内清除缓存或删除应用。 我们无法远程访问或删除这些数据，因为它们从未离开你的设备。

第三方组件

本应用包含 Open CASCADE Technology（LGPL 2.1 + Open CASCADE exception）和 three.js（MIT）。 许可证与第三方通知文本随应用分发，见应用包内的 THIRD_PARTY/ 目录。 这些组件不收集数据。

政策变更

如果本政策发生重大变化，我们会更新本页面并在新版本中说明。

联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：humorwu@163.com

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 2026-11-11 · For the ModelPeek iPhone / iPad app

ModelPeek (the "App") is an offline 3D / CAD model viewer. We keep data handling to a minimum: the App does not collect, upload, or sell any personal data.

What the App processes

  • Model files you choose: used to convert and display the model locally on your device. Files are copied into the App's temporary directory, and converted output is cached in the App's Caches directory. You can clear the cache in the App or delete the App to remove all data.
  • App settings and review notes: stored locally on your device (UserDefaults and the App sandbox) and never uploaded. Review notes leave the device only when you export or share them.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs: kept locally on your device for troubleshooting; the App does not upload them automatically.

What the App does not do

  • No sign-up or sign-in;
  • No collection of device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or usage behavior;
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs;
  • No model or file content sent to third-party servers;
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Network

The App works without a network connection. It reads bundled viewer resources through an internal custom URL scheme and does not access the internet.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect data from children and contains no ads or user-generated content.

Data retention

Model caches and local settings remain on your device until you clear the cache in the App or delete the App. We cannot access or delete this data remotely because it never leaves your device.

Third-party components

The App includes Open CASCADE Technology (LGPL 2.1 with the Open CASCADE exception) and three.js (MIT). Their license and notice texts are distributed with the App under THIRD_PARTY/. These components do not collect data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the change in a new release.
